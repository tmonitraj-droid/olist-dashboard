Findings and recommendations

  • Growth stalled in 2018. Monthly orders sit around 6,000–7,000 since January 2018 after a Black Friday spike of 7,421 in November 2017.
  • One state dominates. São Paulo alone brings 38% of revenue. Growth in Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais is the clearest diversification path.
  • Spread across categories. No single category passes 10% of revenue, which lowers risk. Health and beauty, watches and gifts, and bed and bath lead.
  • Fix delivery before marketing. Cutting late deliveries would lift reviews and repeat purchases, the weakest metric at 3%.
